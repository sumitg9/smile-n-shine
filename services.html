Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about dental treatments in Siliguri.

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful?

No! Root Canal Treatment is performed under effective local anesthesia. In fact, RCT relieves the intense throbbing pain caused by an infected tooth pulp. Most patients report feeling only mild pressure during the procedure.

Does teeth cleaning (scaling) make teeth loose or thin?

This is a common myth. Ultrasonic scaling only removes accumulated tartar (calculus) and harmful bacterial deposits. It does not scrape away or weaken the natural tooth enamel. Cleaning actually prevents bone loss and keeps your teeth firm.

How many visits are needed for a dental crown or bridge?

Generally, 2 visits are required. During visit 1, the tooth is shaped, impressions/measurements are taken, and a temporary cover is placed. Within a few days, your custom lab-fabricated crown is ready to be permanently cemented during visit 2.

Do you accept same-day appointment requests for emergency toothache?

Yes. If you are experiencing severe toothache, swelling, or trauma, please call our clinic directly at +91 74776 86823 and we will do our best to accommodate you immediately during our working hours.
